How It Works

01
Crawl
Playwright fetches fully JS-rendered pages, finds the privacy policy, terms, and cookie pages.
02
Audit
12 DPDP Act 2023 rules are checked using regex, spaCy NLP, and HTML analysis.
03
Verify
Grievance Officer contacts verified via DNS, MX, SMTP, carrier lookup, and Wayback Machine.
04
Report
A weighted risk score (0–100) with A–F grade and a 7-page PDF audit report are generated.
